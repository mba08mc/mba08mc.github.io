About this site:

I initially created this website at some point in 2022 as a site to host my Cydia repository, however it was hardly used and I only had a couple tweaks on there, one of them being my own which I created, iSign, a sort of AppSync Unified but for iPhoneOS 2 but without all the hassle of dealing with plists and permissions. Around 2024-ish I expanded the site and moved to altnet.online from my previous github pages domain. Since then the site has seen a few changes, but remaining mostly stagnant with the odd update every few months.
